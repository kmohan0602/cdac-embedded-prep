# How to Get Previous Year Papers — Step by Step Guide

## Why Papers Aren't Available for Direct Download
CDAC does NOT officially release exam papers. All available papers are:
1. Reconstructed from memory by past students
2. Shared in private Telegram/WhatsApp groups
3. Available through coaching institutes (paid)

---

## Step 1: Telegram Groups (Best Source — Free)
Search these terms on Telegram:
- "CDAC DESD"
- "CDAC embedded preparation"
- "CDAC ACTS embedded"
- "CDAC Pune embedded"

**How to join:**
1. Open Telegram app
2. Search above terms
3. Join groups with 1000+ members
4. Look for pinned messages with question papers
