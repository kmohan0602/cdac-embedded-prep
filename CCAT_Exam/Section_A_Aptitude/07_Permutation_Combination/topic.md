## Key Formulas

Factorial: n! = n × (n-1) × ... × 1

Permutation (order matters): nPr = n! / (n-r)!
Combination (order doesn't matter): nCr = n! / (r! × (n-r)!)

nCr = nC(n-r)
nC0 = nCn = 1

Probability:
P(event) = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)  [if independent]
P(not A) = 1 - P(A)

## Sample Questions

Q1. In how many ways can 4 people sit in a row of 6 chairs?
Answer: 6P4 = 6!/(6-4)! = 360

Q2. Choose 3 from 7 people for a team?
Answer: 7C3 = 35

Q3. Probability of getting heads twice in 2 coin flips?
Answer: P = (1/2)×(1/2) = 1/4
