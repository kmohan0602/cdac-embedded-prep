Practice: https://www.indiabix.com/verbal-ability/sentence-correction/

- Subject-verb agreement
- Tense consistency
- Pronoun reference
- Misplaced modifiers

### Spotting Errors
Practice: https://www.indiabix.com/verbal-ability/spotting-errors/

### Reading Comprehension
Practice: https://www.indiabix.com/verbal-ability/reading-comprehension/
Strategy:
- Read passage once quickly
- Read questions
- Scan passage for answers
- Don't assume — answer only from passage

### One Word Substitution
Practice: https://www.indiabix.com/verbal-ability/one-word-substitutes/

### Ordering of Sentences (Para Jumbles)
Practice: https://www.indiabix.com/verbal-ability/ordering-of-sentences/
Strategy: Find the opening sentence (no reference to previous), find pairs of sentences that connect logically

## Strategy
- Verbal is the easiest to improve with daily 15-minute practice
- Do NOT skip reading comprehension — always 2-3 passages in CCAT
- Spotting errors tests grammar rules — memorize top 10 error types

---

## Target
Do 10 verbal questions per day covering different sub-topics.
