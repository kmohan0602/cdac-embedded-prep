## Key Formulas

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

x% of y = (x × y) / 100

If A is x% more than B → A = B × (1 + x/100)
If A is x% less than B → A = B × (1 - x/100)

% increase = (New - Old) / Old × 100
% decrease = (Old - New) / Old × 100

Successive % change: If two successive changes of a% and b%
→ Net change = a + b + (ab/100)%

## Sample Questions

Q1. What is 35% of 400?
Answer: 140

Q2. A price increases from 200 to 250. What is the % increase?
Answer: (250-200)/200 × 100 = 25%

Q3. A number is increased by 20% then decreased by 20%. Net change?
Answer: 20 + (-20) + (20×(-20)/100) = -4% → decrease of 4%
