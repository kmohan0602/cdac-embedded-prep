## Key Formulas

Speed = Distance / Time
Distance = Speed × Time
Time = Distance / Speed

Unit conversion:
x km/h = x × (5/18) m/s
x m/s  = x × (18/5) km/h

Average Speed (two equal distances at s1 and s2):
= 2×s1×s2 / (s1+s2)   ← NOT (s1+s2)/2

Relative Speed:
- Same direction: s1 - s2
- Opposite direction: s1 + s2

Trains:
- Cross a pole/person: Distance = Length of train
- Cross a platform: Distance = Length of train + Length of platform
- Two trains cross: Distance = Sum of lengths

## Sample Questions

Q1. A train 150m long crosses a pole in 15 seconds. Speed?
Answer: Speed = 150/15 = 10 m/s = 36 km/h

Q2. Train 200m crosses platform 300m at 72 km/h. Time taken?
Answer: 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Distance = 500m. Time = 500/20 = 25 sec

Q3. A and B travel towards each other from 300km apart at 60 and 90 km/h. Meet in?
Answer: Relative speed = 150. Time = 300/150 = 2 hours
